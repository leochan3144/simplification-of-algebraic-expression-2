    Simplify algebraic expressions, one line at a time.

    Eight question types from your worksheet. Write each line of working after the equals sign, just like in your homework, and earn the trophy.

    How it works

    • Each step is a new line starting with =. Type the whole line on the number pad.
    • Correct line: +10. A line that changes the value: −10 and you get a hint.
    • If your line is equal but isn’t the step asked for (for example you skipped grouping), you get a tip and lose nothing.
    • Press Highlight like terms to colour terms that can be collected together.
    • Make a mistake and you’ll get one more question of the same type. Finish a question with no mistakes to clear the type. Clear all 8 to win the trophy.
    • For powers, type the letter, press xn, then the number.
